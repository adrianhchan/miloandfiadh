- Copied the supplied photo unchanged to `assets/images/author-and-milo.jpg` (1729 × 1731, 382,066 bytes). A read-only EXIF inspection found no GPS metadata. Its original bytes were verified with `cmp`.
- Added static, unique titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph/Twitter previews, and JSON-LD on ten public pages. Existing imagery is reused for sharing. The homepage and book pages preload their existing hero poster; no artwork or video was recompressed or replaced.

- Added `robots.txt`, a ten-URL canonical `sitemap.xml`, and a branded non-indexable `404.html`. Robots keep the former unrestricted public crawling policy, including the same default training-crawler access; no new selective AI-training policy was imposed.
- Added `_config.yml` exclusions for the confirmed Jekyll publishing boundary, retaining old files in source while excluding duplicates, development code, and working notes from the public site. A local static preview mirrors those exclusions and the observed extensionless aliases.
- Replaced unconditional Google tags with locally hosted `analytics.js`. The existing measurement ID loads only on the approved production hosts after explicit consent. No request, including a denied-consent ping, is sent to Google before opt-in. Preview hosts never load Google or delete analytics cookies shared with other local projects.
